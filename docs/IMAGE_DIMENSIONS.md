## 1. Page Hero Banners

The large rotating banner at the top of each page. Uploaded per page in **Admin > Site Settings > Hero Images**.

### Home Page Hero (Taller)

| What | Size | Notes |
|------|------|-------|
| Banner image | **2400 × 1000 px** | Very wide rectangle, taller than other pages |

**Where it appears:**
- Home page only (top banner, 500px tall)

### Other Page Heroes

| What | Size | Notes |
|------|------|-------|
| Banner image | **2400 × 600 px** | Very wide rectangle, landscape format |

**Where it appears:**
- Destinations page (top banner)
- Hotels page (top banner)
- Tours page (top banner)
- Cars page (top banner)
- Flights page (top banner)
- Events page (top banner)
- Deals page (top banner)
- Blog page (top banner)
- Visa page (top banner)

## 2. Entity Main Image

The main photo for each item (destination, hotel, tour, car, event, blog post). Uploaded when creating or editing an entity in **Admin**.

| What | Size | Notes |
|------|------|-------|
| Main image | **768 × 512 px** | Landscape format, 3:2 ratio |

**Where it appears:**
- Listing page cards (Destinations, Hotels, Tours, Cars, Events, Blog)
- Home page scrolling cards (same image, reused)
- Home page landing sections (same image, reused)
- Detail page (top of the page)

---

## 3. Entity Gallery Photos

Multiple photos uploaded per entity (hotels, destinations, tours, etc.). Shown on the detail page only.

| What | Size | Notes |
|------|------|-------|
| Gallery photo | **1440 × 720 px** | Wide rectangle, fills most of the screen |

**Where it appears:**
- Detail page only (Destination, Hotel, Tour, Car, Event detail pages)

The colorful promotional banner on the home page. Uploaded in **Admin > Site Settings**.

| What | Size | Notes |
|------|------|-------|

**Where it appears:**

## 5. Site Gallery Photos

Photos for the gallery page. Uploaded in **Admin > Gallery**.

| What | Size | Notes |
|------|------|-------|
| Gallery photo | **Any width, 600+ px height recommended** | Will be auto-cropped to fit columns |

**Where it appears:**
- Gallery page (masonry/column layout)
- Home page scrolling gallery section (same photos, reused)

## 6. Partner Logos

Logos of Belazur Travel's partner companies. Uploaded in **Admin > Partners**.

| What | Size | Notes |
|------|------|-------|
| Partner logo | **640 × 448 px** | Landscape format, will be centered |

**Where it appears:**
- Home page only (scrolling partner logos section)

## 7. Landing Video

The video popup that appears when visitors first open the Belazur Travel homepage. Uploaded in **Admin > Site Settings > Landing Video**.

| What | Size | Notes |
|------|------|-------|
| Portrait video | **1080 × 1920 px** | Like a phone screen (vertical) |
| Landscape video | **1920 × 1080 px** | Like a TV screen (horizontal) |

**Where it appears:**
- Home page only (popup modal)

## Upload Tips

1. **Always upload the largest image possible** — the Belazur Travel website will automatically resize it.
2. **Use landscape (horizontal) format** for most images unless noted otherwise.
3. **Use portrait (vertical) format** only for the landing video.
4. **Keep important content in the center** — edges may be cropped on different screens.
5. **Avoid text in images** — it may be hard to read when resized.
6. **One image = one upload** — the same image is automatically reused across pages (listing cards, home page sections, etc.), so there's no need to re-upload it for each location.
