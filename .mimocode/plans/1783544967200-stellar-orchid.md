# Fix Landing Video Modal - Portrait Video Spacing

## Problem
When a portrait video is shown in the modal on desktop/laptop:
1. The dialog has `max-w-4xl` (896px) so it's much wider than the portrait video
2. Large transparent space appears on left and right of the video
3. The close button (X) sits at `absolute end-4 top-4` relative to the dialog, over the transparent area
4. The mute button is also in empty space

## Root Cause
The dialog takes a fixed max-width regardless of video aspect ratio. With `object-contain`, portrait videos get pillarboxing (side bars), and UI buttons are positioned relative to the dialog container, not the video.

## Solution

### File: `resources/js/components/sections/LandingVideoModal.tsx`

Make the dialog shrink-wrap to the video's actual width instead of forcing a fixed max-width:

**Change 1 - DialogContent (line 44):**
Replace `max-w-full sm:max-w-2xl lg:max-w-4xl` with `w-fit max-w-[90vw]`
- `w-fit`: dialog width = content width (the video), no extra space
- `max-w-[90vw]`: safety net so it never exceeds the viewport

**Change 2 - Video element (line 57):**
Replace `w-full` with `max-w-full`
- Video sizes to its intrinsic aspect ratio, constrained by `max-h-[80vh]` and `max-w-full`
- Portrait video: dialog shrinks to video width, no side gaps
- Landscape video: dialog expands to video width (up to 90vw)

No other files need changes. The close button and mute button positioning is already correct relative to their containers.

## Verification
1. Portrait video on desktop: dialog wraps tightly around the video, no transparent sides
2. Landscape video on desktop: dialog expands to video width, close button on top-right of video
3. Mobile: `max-w-[90vw]` prevents overflow, video scales down
4. Close button and mute button always over the video, not in empty space
